# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Participants must communicate professionally, disagree with evidence rather
than personal attacks, respect confidentiality, and make room for different
backgrounds and levels of experience. Harassment, discrimination, threats,
doxing, sexualized conduct, and disclosure of private or customer information
are unacceptable.

## Project-specific expectations

- Do not pressure contributors to disclose customer evidence, proprietary
  source, model inputs, or embargoed vulnerability details.
- Do not present engineering output as certification, conformity approval, or
  a statement made on behalf of a certification body.
- Raise vulnerability reports privately under `SECURITY.md`.
- Disclose conflicts of interest relevant to a contribution or release.

## Enforcement and contact

Ding Guan (`@guanding`) is the sole maintainer and conduct moderator. There is
no alternate moderator or independent appeal channel. For a non-sensitive
concern, open an issue. For a sensitive concern, open an issue containing no
details and request a private channel. Vulnerabilities must use the private
process in `SECURITY.md`.

The maintainer may edit or remove content, reject contributions, restrict
participation, or exclude participants when necessary to protect people,
evidence, or the project. Decisions should be documented, access-limited, and
proportionate. Retaliation against a good-faith reporter is prohibited.

# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Participants must communicate professionally, disagree with evidence rather
than personal attacks, respect confidentiality, and make room for different
backgrounds and levels of experience. Harassment, discrimination, threats,
doxing, sexualized conduct, and disclosure of private or customer information
are unacceptable.

## Project-specific expectations

- Do not pressure contributors to disclose customer evidence, proprietary
  source, model inputs, or embargoed vulnerability details.
- Do not present engineering output as certification, conformity approval, or
  a statement made on behalf of a certification body.
- Raise security reports privately under `SECURITY.md`.
- Disclose conflicts of interest that could affect a rights or release review.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, reject contributions, temporarily
restrict participation, or permanently exclude participants when necessary to
protect people, evidence, or the project.

Conduct concerns must be reported privately to the repository owner's named
moderation contact. A primary and alternate moderation contact must be
configured before public community participation is enabled. Until then,
public community launch remains blocked; do not put sensitive reports in an
issue.

Enforcement decisions should be documented, access-limited, proportionate,
and independently reviewed when practicable. Retaliation against a good-faith
reporter is prohibited.
